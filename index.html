Career timeline

Ten years across three companies, building secure fintech, healthcare, research, emergency-response and on-demand platforms.

  1. Feb 2023 — Present Fintech

    Senior iOS Developer

    Armup · Pakistan

    I lead secure fintech and mobile-infrastructure work across a crypto exchange and its companion transaction authenticator, both architected with VIPER. I architected and developed the Arm Transaction Authenticator and the secure authentication flows that verify crypto withdrawal transactions, while continuing to maintain and enhance the Armup crypto exchange application.

    Much of my focus is on security and real-time reliability — JWT-based authorization, Face ID biometrics, device binding and multi-factor verification, with sensitive data protected through Keychain Services and encryption best practices. I developed secure mobile-to-mobile transaction verification over Apple Multipeer Connectivity for encrypted peer-to-peer data exchange, and delivered real-time transaction updates through push notifications, deep linking and backend-driven event workflows. Alongside this I build reusable UI components and work closely with design, backend and Android teams on API contracts to improve reliability, scalability and security.

  2. Dec 2019 — Feb 2023 Healthcare · Research · Emergency

    Senior iOS Developer

    Troon Technologies · Islamabad, Pakistan

    I worked cross-functionally on production applications spanning healthcare management, behavioral research and emergency response, building and maintaining native iOS and Android apps across the portfolio — from evidence-based digestive health to medical-grade wearable research and offline-first emergency command centers.

    For behavioral research I integrated the Empatica E4 medical-grade wearable for real-time physiological data collection, implementing automated and deviation-based survey scheduling and custom data-export pipelines. I also engineered offline-first workflows for emergency-response operations in low-connectivity environments, and consistently drove stability improvements, performance optimization and user-experience refinements across the apps I shipped.

    Selected apps — Time2Feel, Health Pro Solutions and MCBCEM — are featured in Featured Work.

  3. Mar 2016 — Dec 2019 On-Demand · E-Commerce

    iOS Developer

    Vizteck Solutions · Islamabad, Pakistan

    I pioneered the company's on-demand application division and architected the reusable iOS frameworks that underpinned it, building scalable components that accelerated the development of each new on-demand product.

    Across the portfolio I delivered real-time tracking, live status updates and user–provider communication using CoreLocation, Google Maps and Socket.IO, and integrated payment gateways, authentication systems and push notifications into the shared framework layer.
